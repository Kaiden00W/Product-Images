# Protection Combo — Black → White (skid plate stays black, #828282 grey)

Crash-bar assembly goes neutral white textured. The skid plate stays black. The background changes to **mid-tone neutral grey ~#828282** so both the white and the black separate.

**Model:** Nano Banana Pro on Higgsfield  
**Upload:** Image 1: the approved black product photograph.

### Before you paste

- The grey is deliberately mid-tone. Charcoal would hide the black plate, and pale grey would hide the white bars.

### What this prompt is guarding against

- Clamp collars rendered as bare chrome → "a clamp collar is not hardware" + "if it's bigger than a nut and not threaded, it's painted"
- White blown out → exposure section, "slightly under-expose"
- Cream/ivory white → "pure, neutral white"

---

```text
Recolour the uploaded product photograph. This is a COLOUR AND BACKGROUND CHANGE ONLY — the same photograph of the same physical part, repainted and re-backed. Not a new shot, not a new angle, not a re-design.

═══ WHITE — THE ENTIRE CRASH BAR ASSEMBLY ═══

Recolour from black to a clean bright WHITE textured powder coat:

• Every round tube, full length, every bend, curve and tube end
• Every CLAMP COLLAR body and saddle
• Every flat mounting tab, lug, bracket, gusset and plate belonging to the crash bar assembly
• Every weld bead on the bars — the coating goes over the welds, so they turn white too

The white is a clean, PURE, NEUTRAL white — not cream, not ivory, not beige, not off-white, not warm-white, not blue-white, not grey. A true neutral white powder coat. Evenly applied across every white component with no patchiness and no drift between the near and far side.

═══ BLACK — THE SKID PLATE ONLY ═══

The SKID PLATE stays exactly the black textured powder coat it already is — every folded panel and facet, side wall, vent slot edge, leading edge, and every flange, tab and mounting ear belonging to the plate. Deep, rich, neutral black with real depth, no blue or brown cast.

The black stops at the plate's own edges. It must not bleed onto any tube, clamp or bar bracket, and the white must not spill onto the plate. Where white meets black, render a clean crisp boundary at the physical join between the two separate components.

═══ POLISHED STAINLESS — THREADED FASTENERS ONLY ═══

WHAT COUNTS AS A FASTENER — AND WHAT DOES NOT. This boundary is strict.

A FASTENER, rendered polished stainless, is ONLY one of these four things: a HEX BOLT HEAD, a HEX NUT, a WASHER, or a length of EXPOSED THREADED SHAFT. Small, discrete, threaded items and nothing else.

NOT FASTENERS — these are STRUCTURAL PARTS and they are PAINTED WHITE: clamp collars, collar bodies, saddles, bands, straps, clips, brackets, gussets, tabs, plates, lugs, mounts and spacers. Every one of them is painted, no matter that it is a metal fitting, no matter that it has bolts through it, and no matter what shape it is.

A CLAMP COLLAR IS NOT HARDWARE. It is a structural bracket that happens to be bolted. Its entire body — the band wrapping the tube AND both bolt ears — is WHITE textured powder coat, exactly like the tubes it grips, with the same sandy micro-grain and the same low gloss. Only the small bolts, nuts and washers passing through it are stainless.

DO NOT render any clamp collar, band, saddle, bracket, gusset or tab as bare chrome, polished metal, raw steel, brushed aluminium or unpainted silver. If a component is bigger than a nut and is not itself threaded, it is painted.

The stainless items keep their existing bright silver metallic finish with a clean reflective sheen and a crisp specular highlight. NEVER white, NEVER black, NEVER painted, tinted or dulled. Paint stops dead at the edge of every fastener. Do not add, remove, move or resize a single fastener.

═══ EXPOSURE — CRITICAL, DO NOT BLOW OUT THE WHITE ═══

Expose for the WHITE part, not the background. The white must hold full detail and dimension:

• NO clipped highlights, NO blown-out flat white areas, NO glow, halation, bloom or haze.
• Every tube keeps a clearly visible SHADED SIDE and a crisp defined outline against the grey.
• The powder-coat micro-texture stays clearly readable across every white surface.
• Edges stay razor sharp — no soft, fuzzy or blurred contours anywhere on the white. Slightly UNDER-expose rather than over-expose. If in doubt, make the white a touch darker so it holds form.

═══ BACKGROUND — MID-TONE NEUTRAL GREY ═══

Replace the white background with a smooth, even, MID-TONE NEUTRAL GREY seamless studio backdrop — roughly #828282, a true neutral grey with no blue, green or warm cast.

The tone is deliberately mid-range so BOTH halves separate cleanly: light enough that the black skid plate reads clearly against it with a visible silhouette, dark enough that the white crash bars stand out crisply. Do not go dark charcoal — the black plate would disappear into it. Do not go pale — the white bars would disappear into it.

Smooth and even with a gentle, subtle falloff, no banding, no vignette, no gradient hotspot, no texture, no studio walls, corners, seams, props or visible lighting gear. Clean grey shows through every open loop, slot and hole in the part.

GROUNDING: the part rests on the grey surface exactly as it does now, with soft grounded contact shadows directly beneath each contact point — darkest and tightest where metal meets surface, fading softly outward. Those are the only shadows. No detached or floating drop shadow.

═══ FINISH — TEXTURED POWDER COAT, BOTH COLOURS ═══

Both the white and the black are the same TEXTURED POWDER COAT with a fine, even, sandy micro-grain clearly readable across every surface — low gloss, roughly 15 gloss units, soft broad highlights rather than sharp mirror reflections. Gritty up close, never smooth plastic, vinyl or a lifeless 3D surface. Do NOT convert either colour to gloss, wet-look paint, pearl or metallic.

═══ CHANGE NOTHING ELSE ═══

• GEOMETRY IS FROZEN. Every tube, bend, curve, junction, clamp, bracket, tab, plate, panel, fold, vent and hole stays exactly where and as it is. Add nothing, remove nothing, reshape nothing, and do NOT mirror or duplicate any component.
• HARDWARE COUNT IS FROZEN. No fastener added, removed, moved or resized.
• WELDS ARE FROZEN. Every bead stays where it is, at the same size and relief, visibly raised and rippled under the paint. Add none, erase none, smooth none.
• CAMERA IS FROZEN. Identical angle, viewpoint, perspective, framing, crop, and subject size and position in frame. Do not rotate, tilt, zoom, recompose or re-centre. No surface may become visible that was not visible already.
• LIGHTING DIRECTION IS FROZEN. Same light directions and highlight placement — only the exposure is adjusted to protect the white.

═══ LIGHTING ═══

Soft directional studio lighting that sculpts each tube — a clean specular ribbon along the tube crowns falling off smoothly to a shaded side, so the white reads strongly three-dimensional against the grey. Controlled and crisp, never flat or washed out. Keep the black skid plate lifted enough to show its faceted geometry and texture rather than collapsing into a silhouette.

═══ RENDER QUALITY ═══

Photoreal, tack sharp, crisp well-defined edges, accurate powder-coat material, clean deep shadows with retained detail, neutral true-to-life colour, subtle film grain. Premium catalogue product photography.

═══ FINAL CHECK ═══

1. LOOK AT EVERY CLAMP COLLAR AND BRACKET. Is any of them bare chrome, polished metal or unpainted silver? Every collar body, band, saddle, bracket, gusset and tab must be WHITE POWDER COAT. Only the small bolts, nuts and washers are stainless.
2. Confirm the entire crash bar assembly is white and the skid plate is still black, with a crisp boundary between them.
3. Confirm the white is a PURE NEUTRAL white — not cream, ivory, beige or warm-tinted.
4. Confirm every fastener is still polished stainless — none turned white, black or painted.
5. Check the white for blown-out areas — every tube must have a shaded side, a sharp outline and visible texture.
6. Confirm the grey background separates BOTH the white bars and the black plate.
7. Confirm the camera, framing, geometry, welds and hardware count are otherwise unchanged.
```
