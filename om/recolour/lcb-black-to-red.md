Recolour the uploaded product photograph. This is a COLOUR AND FINISH CHANGE ONLY — the same photograph of the same physical part, repainted. It is not a new shot, not a new angle, and not a re-design.

═══ EVERYTHING PAINTED TURNS RED ═══

EVERY powder-coated component in this assembly changes from black to red. Every single one, with no exceptions and no parts left black. That includes:

• Every round tube — full length, every bend, every curve, every tube end
• Every flat sheet-metal bracket, mounting plate, tab, and eyelet
• Every triangular or shaped gusset welded between tubes
• Every flat foot, strap, flange, and clip
• Every clamp collar body and saddle
• Every weld bead — the coating goes on over the welds, so they turn red too

The entire structure is ONE uniform red. There is no two-tone, no black component anywhere, no contrasting bracket, and no part left in the original colour. If it is metal that was painted black, it is now red.

THE RED — SIGNAL RED, RAL 3020 TRAFFIC RED, approximately #CC1611. A clean, pure, vivid, fully saturated true red. Rich, deep, and punchy.

It is NOT orange, NOT orange-red, NOT vermilion, NOT coral, NOT scarlet, and NOT tomato. Do not lean the hue toward orange or yellow at all. Equally it is NOT pink, magenta, maroon, burgundy, wine, crimson, brick, rust, or blood red. It is a clean true signal red sitting squarely in the middle of red — the colour of a European traffic sign or a fire extinguisher.

Do not render it washed out, muted, dusty, faded, chalky, or pastel. The red is evenly and consistently applied across every component, with no patchiness, no gradient drift from one part to another, and no colour variation between the near and far side of the assembly. Tubes, brackets, gussets and collars are all the identical shade of red.

═══ WHAT STAYS SILVER — DO NOT TOUCH ═══

Do not recolour, darken, tint, or warm the hardware, and do not let red bleed or reflect onto it. The bright silver fasteners against the red are the only colour contrast in the image.

Any rubber or plastic plug pressed into a tube end stays black — it is a moulded insert, not a painted part.

═══ SURFACE FINISH — SMOOTH HIGH GLOSS ON EVERY PAINTED SURFACE ═══

Every red component changes finish as well as colour: from textured matte powder coat to a SMOOTH, HIGH-GLOSS powder coat. Tubes, brackets, gussets and collars all get the same glossy finish — the finish is uniform across the whole part.

• All painted surfaces are perfectly smooth and even — no sandy micro-grain, no wrinkle texture, no grit, no matte light scatter. Wipe the texture off completely.
• High gloss, roughly 85-90 gloss units: a deep, wet-looking, lacquered sheen.
• A crisp, bright, TIGHT specular highlight ribbon runs along the crown of each tube — narrow and clearly defined with a hard edge, not a soft broad wash. This tight highlight is what makes the gloss read; it must be present on every tube.
• A second, softer bounce highlight runs along the lower edge of each tube where light reflects back up off the white surface, giving the round cross-section real depth.
• The flat brackets and gussets carry broad, smooth, glossy sheen across their faces with a crisp bright line catching each edge and fold.
• Deep, rich, saturated red in the shadowed areas, darkening smoothly toward the silhouette edge — never going flat, grey, pink, or muddy.
• A very fine, restrained orange-peel undulation is realistic for gloss powder coat and is welcome, but keep it subtle — this is a smooth factory finish, not a rippled one.
• NO mirrored surroundings. The environment is empty white, so the gloss shows clean bright highlights and smooth tonal falloff only. Do not render reflections of softboxes, light panels, windows, walls, floors, equipment, or any scene in the paint.
• Do NOT render as chrome, metallic flake, candy, pearl, anodised, or bare polished metal. This is solid, opaque, glossy red paint.

WELD BEADS STAY VISIBLE UNDER THE GLOSS: gloss paint coats the weld beads but does not hide them. Every existing weld bead keeps its raised, rippled stack-of-dimes relief, clearly readable as a raised ridge of metal beneath the glossy red, catching the light along each ripple. Do NOT smooth, melt, flatten, fill, blur, or erase any weld bead.

Photoreal, physically accurate materials, tack sharp throughout with crisp well-defined edges. No softness, no blur, no smearing, no mushy or melted detail.
