# KTM Protection Combo — CAD → Photo (three finishes)

Crash bars + skid plate straight from CAD into the KTM colourway: **KTM orange** tubular frame, **black** bolt-on centre bracket, crossbar and skid plate, **polished stainless** fasteners, on pure white.

**Model:** Nano Banana Pro on Higgsfield

### Before you paste

- The CAD is a single colour, so this prompt identifies hardware **by shape, not colour**. That's the opposite of the silver test used in the other prompts. Don't mix the two.
- Hard-codes that **the rear skid plate mount bracket appears once**, i.e. the part is asymmetric. Confirm on this CAD.

### What this prompt is guarding against

- Clamp bolts rendered orange along with the collar → "a clamp is two things at one spot" (the most repeated error)
- Bolts hanging in open air → "no floating hardware — every fastener is captured"
- A mirrored twin of the rear skid-plate bracket → "never mirror, duplicate or add a component"
- Camera re-angled for a "nicer" shot → the no-new-surfaces test
- Clamp collars rendered as open forks/C-shapes → "complete closed ring, two bolt ears"
- Tubes merging at crowded junctions → depth-order instruction
